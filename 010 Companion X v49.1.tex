\begin{abstract}
This Companion develops the ultraviolet sector of RDCC in its corrected and
Phase-1-integrated v49.1 form. The inter-sector coupling between the visible
and mirror sectors is encoded in the operator $T^{(+)}_{\mu\nu}T^{(-)\mu\nu}$,
which is consistently treated as a dimension-8 operator. This correction
restores EFT coherence, fixes the scaling of the relaxation parameter
$\alpha(t)$, and determines the ultraviolet suppression scale $M$. We derive
the ultraviolet structure of the effective action, its impact on infrared
relaxation, and its role in the tensor and thermodynamic sectors.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}

RDCC describes the Universe as a globally coherent CPT-symmetric quantum state
defined on a bipartite Hilbert space,
\begin{equation}
\mathcal{H} = \mathcal{H}_{(+)} \otimes \mathcal{H}_{(-)}.
\end{equation}
The visible and mirror sectors arise as reduced density matrices of a single
global state and interact only through gravity.

The ultraviolet sector encodes the microscopic origin of this interaction. In
RDCC, the coupling between the sectors is described by the operator
\begin{equation}
\mathcal{O}_{\mathrm{int}} = T^{(+)}_{\mu\nu} T^{(-)\mu\nu},
\end{equation}
which couples the energy-momentum tensors of both sectors through the global
metric.

In earlier versions (up to v48.0), this operator was implicitly treated as a
dimension-6 operator. In v49.1, this interpretation is corrected: the operator
is consistently treated as a dimension-8 operator. This correction is crucial
for EFT coherence and propagates through the relaxation sector, the tensor
sector, and the thermodynamic sector.

\section[Dimension-8 Operator]{Dimension-8 Operator and Effective Action}

The energy-momentum tensor has mass dimension
\begin{equation}
[T_{\mu\nu}] = 4.
\end{equation}
Therefore, the inter-sector operator has mass dimension
\begin{equation}
[T^{(+)}_{\mu\nu} T^{(-)\mu\nu}] = 8.
\end{equation}

The corresponding term in the effective action is
\begin{equation}
S_{\mathrm{int}}
=
\int d^4x \sqrt{-g} \,
\frac{1}{M^4} \,
T^{(+)}_{\mu\nu} T^{(-)\mu\nu},
\label{eq:dim8action}
\end{equation}
where $M$ is the ultraviolet suppression scale. The factor $1/M^4$ ensures
that the operator is properly normalized in the EFT sense.

This term is the unique leading inter-sector coupling consistent with:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=0.8cm]
  \item general covariance,
  \item CPT symmetry,
  \item the bipartite Hilbert space structure,
  \item the absence of direct non-gravitational interactions.
\end{itemize}

\subsection[Possible UV Completion]{Possible UV Completion: Massive Spin-2 Mediator}

The dimension-8 operator
\begin{equation}
T^{(+)}_{\mu\nu} T^{(-)\mu\nu}
\end{equation}
can be interpreted as the infrared footprint of a heavy ultraviolet mediator.
In effective field theory, operators that couple two energy-momentum tensors
typically arise from integrating out a massive spin-2 field at tree level.

A massive spin-2 state $X_{\mu\nu}$ couples universally to the energy-momentum
tensor,
\begin{equation}
\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{int}}
=
\frac{1}{M} X_{\mu\nu}
\left(
T^{(+)\mu\nu}
+
T^{(-)\mu\nu}
\right),
\end{equation}
where $M$ is the mass of the mediator. Integrating out $X_{\mu\nu}$ at tree
level generates the effective operator
\begin{equation}
\mathcal{O}_{\mathrm{eff}}
=
\frac{1}{M^4}
T^{(+)}_{\mu\nu} T^{(-)\mu\nu},
\end{equation}
consistent with the dimension-8 structure used in RDCC.

This interpretation is optional and does not affect the infrared physics of
RDCC. It provides a natural ultraviolet origin for the operator, compatible
with:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=0.8cm]
  \item general covariance,
  \item CPT symmetry,
  \item the bipartite Hilbert space structure,
  \item and the geometric nature of the global metric.
\end{itemize}

A massive spin-2 mediator may be viewed as a Kaluza-Klein graviton, a geometric
resonance, or a geon-like excitation of the ultraviolet gravitational sector.
RDCC does not require a specific ultraviolet completion, but this mechanism
illustrates how the dimension-8 operator can arise microscopically.

\section[Ultraviolet Scale]{Ultraviolet Scale and EFT Coherence}

The scale $M$ parametrizes the strength of the inter-sector coupling. It is
assumed to be much larger than all cosmological energy scales,
\begin{equation}
M \gg H_0, \quad M \gg T_{\mathrm{CMB}}, \quad M \gg \Lambda^{1/4}.
\end{equation}

The dimension-8 suppression ensures that:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=0.8cm]
  \item the coupling is negligible at high energies,
  \item the infrared response is small but non-zero,
  \item the EFT expansion remains valid.
\end{itemize}

The relaxation parameter $\alpha(t)$ scales as
\begin{equation}
\alpha(t) \propto \frac{1}{M^4},
\end{equation}
consistent with the dimension-8 nature of the operator.

The ultraviolet sector is directly connected to the relaxation sector

\begin{equation}
\alpha(t) = \frac{1}{M^4} \, \mathcal{G}(t),
\end{equation}
where $\mathcal{G}(t)$ encodes the infrared geometry of the global state. The
infrared fixpoint is
\begin{equation}
\alpha_{\mathrm{IR}} = \lim_{t \to \infty} \alpha(t),
\end{equation}
and controls all deviations from $\Lambda$CDM.

\subsection[Tensor Sector]{Tensor Sector}

The tensor sector receives a quadratic suppression and logarithmic modulation,
\begin{equation}
\Omega_{\mathrm{GW}} \propto \alpha_{\mathrm{IR}}^2,
\end{equation}
with
\begin{equation}
\Omega_{\mathrm{GW}}(k)
=
\Omega_{\mathrm{GW}}^{(0)}(k)
\left[ 1 + \alpha_{\mathrm{IR}} \log(k/k_0) \right].
\end{equation}

These signatures arise from the global coherence of tensor modes and the
dimension-8 nature of the inter-sector coupling.

\section{Conclusion}

This Companion develops the ultraviolet sector of RDCC in its v49.1 form. The
inter-sector coupling between the visible and mirror sectors is encoded in the
dimension-8 operator $T^{(+)}_{\mu\nu}T^{(-)\mu\nu}$, with suppression scale
$M$. This correction restores EFT coherence, fixes the scaling of the
relaxation parameter $\alpha(t)$, and determines the ultraviolet origin of the
tensor and thermodynamic modifications.

These results integrate the relaxation sector (Companion III and VI), the
tensor sector (Companion II and XI), the thermodynamic sector (Companion IV),
and the Boltzmann hierarchy (Companion VII). They form the ultraviolet
backbone of RDCC and ensure that the framework is consistent as an effective
field theory.
